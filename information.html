Agriculture information

Practical guidance for the Mathura belt — written to be read quickly in the field

Farming tips

Irrigation

Water only when the soil needs it

Check soil moisture before irrigation to avoid unnecessary water consumption. Push a finger two inches into the soil; if it feels damp, wait a day.

  • Irrigate early morning or after sunset
  • Keep field channels clean before each watering
  • Wheat needs water at crown root, tillering and grain filling

Soil management

Test the soil before you buy fertiliser

A soil test once every three years tells you what the field is short of. Buying urea by habit costs more and does less.

  • Collect samples from five spots in the field
  • Mix them and send 500 g to the testing centre
  • Add organic manure before the Rabi sowing

Seasonal farming

Sow within the recommended window

Late sowing is the most common reason for low wheat yield in this belt. The window matters more than the seed rate.

  • Wheat: 10 to 25 November
  • Mustard: first fortnight of October
  • Paddy transplanting: last week of June

Modern practices

Start with one small change

Drip irrigation, laser levelling and zero-till drills all pay back, but trying everything in one season is how farmers lose money.

  • Laser levelling saves about 20% water
  • Zero-till drills cut sowing cost per acre
  • Keep a written record of what you tried

Seasonal farming

Rabi

October to March

Sown after the monsoon and harvested in spring. Wheat, mustard, potato and gram belong here.

Kharif

June to October

Monsoon crops that depend on rainfall. Rice, maize and sugarcane planting falls in this season.

Zaid

March to June

The short summer season between Rabi and Kharif, used for vegetables, melons and fodder.

Irrigation tips

  • Level the field before the season — uneven land wastes both water and fertiliser.
  • Line or pipe the main channel; open earthen channels lose a large share on the way.
  • Drip and sprinkler systems suit vegetables and orchards more than flooded paddy.
  • Stop irrigation about ten days before wheat harvest so the grain dries evenly.
  • Record the date of every watering. It is the cheapest way to spot over-irrigation.

Soil management

  • Add 4 to 5 tonnes of well-rotted farmyard manure per acre before Rabi sowing.
  • Rotate cereals with pulses; one season of gram or moong restores nitrogen.
  • Do not burn crop residue — it destroys the organic matter you paid for.
  • Watch for white salt patches, which mean the soil needs gypsum and better drainage.
  • Keep the soil health card from the testing centre with your land papers.

Modern farming practices

Laser land levelling

Saves roughly a fifth of the irrigation water and gives a more even crop stand. Machines can be hired through the block office.

Zero-till sowing

Sowing wheat directly into paddy stubble cuts fuel and labour cost, and lets sowing happen within the recommended window.

Soil health card

Fertiliser doses based on a test, not habit, usually reduce urea spend without reducing yield.
