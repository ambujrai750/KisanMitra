Frequently asked questions

Who can register on Kisan Mitra?

Any farmer in the block can register, and staff of the block agriculture office can register as management users. Account approval is handled at the office.

How do I add a farmer to the register?

Open Farmers from the sidebar and choose Add farmer. In Phase 1 the form is an interface only, so nothing is saved yet.

Can I book equipment through the platform?

Not yet. The equipment page shows what the block owns and whether it is free, in use or under maintenance. Booking is planned for a later phase.

Are the prices on the products page final?

No. They are sample rates for the 2025 Rabi season, shown to demonstrate the catalogue. There is no cart or payment in this build.

Is the weather information live?

No. The dashboard shows a fixed sample reading. Connecting a live weather service is a later-phase task.

Where does the data on these screens come from?

All figures are sample data written into the HTML. No database or server is connected in Phase 1.

Support information

Field officers

Each village cluster has a field officer who visits weekly. Ask at the block office for the officer assigned to your village.

Record corrections

Bring the land record and an identity document to the office to correct a farmer entry. Corrections take about three working days.

Training sessions

Short sessions on irrigation, soil testing and machine handling run on the first Saturday of every month at Krishi Bhawan.
